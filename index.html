The Great Reveal

Forthcoming

Maud Piray, an American transplant in a near-future Geneva, has built a cushy but predictable life facilitating tiny disagreements on her Small Matters Resolved livestream. Until one day when the Swiss government suddenly demands she lead a session related to its landmark “Caviar Tax” on billionaires, and she’s catapulted from comfort zone to chaos. Over the next three days, Maud surmounts an on-air character sniping, closes a high-stakes deal abroad to head off her producer’s mutiny, and survives a sandstorm in a bossy, self-driving car. Then she gets caught in a tax-related scandal that puts all at risk and makes her a target of anti-Tax extremists. Shaken, her board insists she co-facilitate her programs with a robot—but their plan is interrupted by the world’s biggest news.

The Great Reveal asks: In the marathon of moral endurance, how much of the common good can one person carry and what must they leave behind?

A story in which today’s headlines—wealth taxes, the climate, democratic backsliding and AI displacement—are all too real.

Seeing Green

2012

Arcani Kirsch is on a mission. The world has other ideas.

Fresh out of grad school, Arcani leaves her west coast cocoon to join the staff of a DC-based association of green-minded businesses that, like her, want to see the 1992 Earth Treaty ratified. The first step is to get the new US President to a Ministerial meeting in Paris. But the path to Paris is muddied by an ultranationalist professor who’s plagiarized her work, office lust, an albatross of a sister, and two disruptive exes. Still, Arcani plows ahead, trying to keep her vision true, her methods kosher, and her doting aunt proud. Seeing Green explores the lighter side of making one’s mark on a change-resistant world.
