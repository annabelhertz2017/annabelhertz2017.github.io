Books

Seeing Green

Novel · 2012

Arcani Kirsch is certain she's right. The world has other plans.

A recent graduate with a mission heads to Washington, DC, determined to move an international treaty and, along the way, everyone around her. Seeing Green is a funny, fast-moving novel about idealism, international and office politics, and the rocky, rewarding terrain of family and love, told through a multicultural young woman with a lot to learn.

About

Annabel Lille Hertz is the author of Seeing Green, the story of a young activist's jaunt into the world of environmentalism. Drawing on her background with NGOs, her writing engages readers in international politics and shows the many sides of the people who work in that world.
